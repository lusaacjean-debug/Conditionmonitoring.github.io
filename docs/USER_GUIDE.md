# User guide — inspectors and supervisors

## Open the right checklist
* **From the work order** — tap / copy the CM Inspect link in the Pronto task text (`lusaacjean-debug.github.io/cm/?pm=…`).
* **From a QR label** on the equipment — the tag is filled in automatically.
* **From the home page** — search (e.g. "pump", "PSV", "1000 h") or pick a discipline.

## Complete it
1. Header: date, WO number (or scan the WO QR code), inspector, reviewer, equipment tag, methods.
2. Open **Inspection conditions & traceability**: operating condition and load, your qualification, instrument and serial number, calibration due date (remembered for next time).
3. Each check point: **OK**, **NOT OK** or **N/A** against the acceptance criterion shown under it.
4. **NOT OK** needs a finding and a corrective action. Raise a Pronto **work request**, type its number in *Corrective action / WR No.* and set the **priority**: P1 Immediate · P2 ≤ 7 days · P3 ≤ 30 days · P4 Next shutdown.
5. Enter readings, thickness (remaining life is calculated) and photos where asked.
6. The draft saves automatically on the device.

## Report and close
1. **Download PDF** — answer the *pre-issue review* points first (missing data, reviewer, calibration, readings, open defects) — file name starts with the WO number.
2. Pronto → Work Orders → open the WO → **Attached** → add the PDF.
3. Enter hours and a short comment (health %, WR numbers), complete the WO.

**Rule: no PDF attached = job not finished.**

## Referencing a defect
Quote the document number and item, e.g. *"CMI-ROT-025 item 4.2 — seal leaking"*. The same number is on screen, in print and in the PDF.

## Printing
Use **Print** (A4). The print-out shows every section expanded, only the selected result, and a sign-off table.

## Offline
After the first visit the site works without network. Drafts stay on the device; download the PDF when back in coverage if needed.
