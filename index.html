How an inspection works

The same three steps on a phone in the field or a PC in the office.

Select the asset

Search or pick the discipline, open the checklist, and enter the tag, work order (or scan its QR code) and inspector.

Inspect and record

Mark each point OK, NOT OK or N/A against its acceptance standard. NOT OK points need a finding and a corrective action. Add readings, thickness and photos. Everything saves as you go.

Report and act

Download the PDF report with defects listed first, the health score and remaining life, and a sign-off block. Raise each defect as a Pronto work request.
